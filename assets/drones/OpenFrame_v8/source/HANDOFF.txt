OPEN-FRAME 7-INCH DRONE — V8 — PROJECTAIRSIM HANDOFF

Replaces the previous open-frame revision. Seven-inch propellers retained at the user's
request; do not use the old nine-inch/0.114 m radius or 0.253 m motor offsets.

WHAT CHANGED
Reference-inspired revision: shallow plates with a 22.25 mm clear gap,
compact integrated camera nose, fuller electronics bay with a separate ESC,
flight controller, video transmitter and receiver. Dark stepped outrunner
motors have cylindrical bells, tapered tops and cooling-slot detail.
The taller blue shrink-wrapped battery has a lightly uneven surface, a folded
end seam, one central strap, connected power leads and a secured balance connector.
The capped rear mast leans backward about 21.7 degrees from vertical.
Its narrow boot sits directly on the upper rear plate between the two silver
screws. The projecting rear bracket has been removed.
Main power uses two mated connector halves with no exposed contacts. The
balance/charging plug is secured on the far side beneath the central strap;
it is not a flight power connection.
Seven-inch prop diameter, rotor X/Y coordinates, and frame origin remain
unchanged. Rotor hubs are raised by 4 mm: NEW BODY-FRAME Z = -0.006 m.
Update the prior -0.010 m rotor Z value in your configuration. Small motor
shafts bridge the hub gap; the blades clear the motor housings by at least
1.9 mm for every rotation angle.

UNITS, AXES, AND WINDING — DO NOT CONVERT TWICE
All glTF numeric positions are METRES; all scales are 1.
GLBs follow glTF 2.0: right-handed, +Y up, CCW front-face winding.
This asset faces +X. Its native glTF rotor spin axis is +Y.
Source Blender axes: right-handed, +X forward, +Y left, +Z up.
The exporter maps source (x,y,z) to glTF (x,z,-y), a proper rotation.
Normals and winding have already been exported for this coordinate system.

For the requested Unreal-style Z-up body frame (+X forward, +Y right):
  source -> requested frame: (x, -y, z)
  native glTF -> requested frame: (x, z, y)
  multiply metre coordinates by 100 only when assigning Unreal centimetres.
The requested-frame conversion includes a handedness reflection. A custom
mesh loader applying it must also handle normals and reverse triangle winding
exactly once. If your Unreal glTF importer already converts handedness/units,
use its result and do not repeat this transform. Keep rotor spin axes local.

ROTATION
Every Prop_* node origin is at the hub center. The CW and CCW blade shapes
are mirrored, independent closed meshes. No animation is baked.
Native GLB: rotate each prop about LOCAL Y.
After conversion to Z-up: rotate each prop about LOCAL Z.
Angle per frame in degrees = RPM * 6 * delta_seconds. Choose opposite signs
for the two handedness groups in your runtime's rotation convention.
The motor housings are stationary; the hubs and nuts rotate with the blades.

MOUNTS AND ORIGIN
Root/body origin is the geometric frame center at (0,0,0), not a calculated
center of mass. Bind the physics origin to this frame or explicitly provide
your own mass/inertia offset; do not recenter each visual part independently.

Mount_Camera: source/requested body position (0.100, 0, -0.024) m.
Optical direction +X; pitch/yaw/roll = 0 degrees. Native GLB node position
is (0.100, -0.024, 0). This is a mount Empty, not a glTF Camera object.
Mount_LED: source/requested position (0.106, 0, -0.035) m; direction +X.
Native GLB position (0.106, -0.035, 0). No emissive light is included.

COLLISION
Separate body box center (0.005,0,0.030) m, size (0.280,0.105,0.180) m
in source/requested axes. Encloses central frame, battery, and camera area. The slender rear antenna is excluded from collision.
It is a proxy asset, not automatically configured Unreal collision.
No rotor collision. Narrow arms extend beyond the central box.

THERMAL / MATERIALS
Map Battery to your warm battery class; Motor_FL, Motor_FR, Motor_RL,
Motor_RR to your warm motor class. Names remain identifiable in LOD1.
ESC is a separate connected closed mesh including its PCB, MOSFETs and
solder pads. Map the exact node name ESC to the warm ESC class; the separate
ESC_Capacitor may also be classified warm if desired. FlightController is
separate. Motor phase leads and other wires should not inherit motor heat
merely because their names include the motor prefix.
Battery geometry is nominally 0.145 x 0.058 x 0.093 m, centered at source
(-0.017, 0, 0.0395) m. The central strap and folded-wrap seam are separate closed meshes.
Other small hardware remains individually named and editable.
VideoTransmitter and Receiver identify the extra populated boards.
Antenna_Mast, Antenna_Cap, Antenna_Mount and
Antenna_Coax identify the
single rear antenna assembly. It is a visual element with no RF simulation.
Most frame/electronics base colours remain subdued. Material-specific
values for cloth and metals now supersede the original blanket albedo range. The user-requested vivid blue battery is an explicit
exception: Battery_Blue_Shrinkwrap is (0.006,0.12,0.78), FoldedWrap is
(0.009,0.10,0.60). Its slightly uneven geometry exceeds its nominal envelope
by around 1–2 millimeters. Battery wrap now has a glossy shrink-film finish: roughness 0.18 on the
main wrap and 0.22 on the end fold, with specular 0.5 and a thin clearcoat
(weight 0.35, roughness 0.10–0.12). Colour and geometry are unchanged.
Metallic is 1 for the silver hardware, 0.85 for motor casings, and 0 for the
cloth, carbon and other non-metallic components.
Beauty discs use alpha 0.13 and are isolated in their own file.
Visibility at 100 m depends on camera resolution, lighting, and background;
the model deliberately avoids bright metallic surfaces.

V8 MATERIAL NOTES
- Velcro now has near-black linear albedo 0.0025–0.007, roughness 1.0,
  specular 0 and no sheen or clearcoat. Its loop/fibre normal map is stronger.
  This deliberately replaces the old 0.05 minimum albedo for the strap.
- Carbon tow size is now 6 mm, THREE TIMES the prior 2 mm. The tile is 24 mm.
  Weave is on broad laminate faces; cut edges retain a plain dark finish.
- Motor_Anodized uses base colour (0.018,0.022,0.028), metallic 0.85,
  roughness 0.36: a dark anodized-aluminium approximation.
- Dull_Hardware is now genuinely metallic: base (0.55,0.58,0.62), metallic 1,
  roughness 0.28. This shared material covers the silver screws, standoffs,
  shafts, camera bolts and other exposed silver hardware.
- The battery wrap is now glossy/lacquer-like, as described above.
  Props, rotor coordinates, clearance, and all other materials are unchanged.
